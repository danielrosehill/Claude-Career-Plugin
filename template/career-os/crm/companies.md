# Companies

Master list of every company on radar — researched, applied-to, contacted, watching, or passed-on. The full outreach log is `outreach.md`; the pipeline view is `opportunities.md`.

| slug | name | domain | lens | status | last-touch | research-brief | notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Status values

- `watching` — interesting, no contact yet.
- `researched` — brief written, no contact.
- `engaged` — outreach sent, awaiting reply.
- `active` — in conversation / interviewing / contracted.
- `paused` — stalled but worth re-trying later.
- `passed` — decided no.

## Lens values

- `employer` — potential employer.
- `client` — potential consulting client.
- `partner` — collaborator / introducer / not directly transactional.
