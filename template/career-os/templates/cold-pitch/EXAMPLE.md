# Cold Pitch — example

Use for first-touch outreach to someone who has no prior context on you. Lead with their problem; mention yourself only enough to establish credibility.

## Variables

- `{{contact_first_name}}`
- `{{company_name}}`
- `{{specific_observation}}` — one concrete thing about their work / company you noticed.
- `{{your_relevance}}` — one line on why you're plausibly useful here.
- `{{ask}}` — short, single, low-stakes (e.g. "20 min next week to compare notes").
- `{{your_signature}}`

## Body

Subject: {{specific_observation}} — quick question

Hi {{contact_first_name}},

I came across {{company_name}} after {{specific_observation}}. {{your_relevance}}.

I'm not pitching anything specific — just curious whether {{topic_of_their_work}} is something your team is currently grappling with, and if so whether a quick conversation would be useful for both sides.

If a 20-minute call next week works, here are a couple of slots: {{slot_1}} / {{slot_2}}. Otherwise no worries — happy to leave it here.

{{your_signature}}

## Notes for the agent

- Cite a real, recent thing — never invented. Pull from research brief.
- Don't compliment generically. "Great company" / "impressive growth" → cut.
- Single ask. Don't bundle.
- If consulting lens, don't pitch the package in the first email.
