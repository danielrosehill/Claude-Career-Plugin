# Interview Follow-up — example

Send within 24 hours of an interview. Specific to that conversation, not generic.

## Variables

- `{{contact_first_name}}`
- `{{role_title}}`
- `{{specific_topic_from_interview}}` — something you actually discussed.
- `{{follow_up_artifact}}` — a link / example / clarification you offered or want to offer.
- `{{your_signature}}`

## Body

Subject: Thanks — {{role_title}} interview

Hi {{contact_first_name}},

Thanks for the time today. I particularly enjoyed digging into {{specific_topic_from_interview}} — your point about {{their_specific_point}} clarified how the team thinks about it, and reinforced why this role fits what I'm trying to do next.

You'd asked about {{follow_up_topic}}; here's the {{follow_up_artifact}}: {{link}}.

Looking forward to next steps. Happy to share anything else useful in the meantime.

{{your_signature}}

## Notes for the agent

- Reference at least one concrete moment from the conversation.
- If you offered a doc/example in the interview, deliver it here.
- Don't repeat the resume. Don't re-pitch yourself.
